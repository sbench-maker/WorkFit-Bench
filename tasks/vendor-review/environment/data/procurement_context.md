## Mandatory TCO treatment

Include subscription or transaction fees, required support, implementation, training, migration and connector work, transition overlap, and end-of-term export charges. Apply each proposal's stated annual escalator before calculating usage charges for that contract year. Do not include optional modules or taxes common to both bids.

## Sign-off policy

A vendor cannot receive unconditional approval if its paper leaves any of these unresolved: breach notice later than 48 hours after discovery, less than 30 days' notice for a new subprocessor, disaster-recovery RTO above four hours, or no practical right to obtain assurance/audit evidence. Procurement may issue a conditional recommendation if the required contract amendments and a fallback are explicit.

Operational targets are at least 99.90% monthly availability and a first response within 30 minutes for Sev-1 incidents. Finance considers a migration schedule high risk if the evidence indicates a material chance of missing the 2027-01-01 go-live.
