# H2 portfolio planning brief

This is a fictional, frozen planning packet for the 2026-09-09 portfolio review. Use the three horizons below rather than inventing calendar precision:

- **Now:** 2026-09-14 through 2026-10-11.
- **Next:** 2026-10-12 through 2026-12-06.
- **Later:** 2026-12-07 through 2027-01-17.

The weekly capacity file is already net of support, leave, meetings, and incident reserve. Sum it by team and horizon. Each initiative's remaining points must fit wholly in one horizon; completed work has zero remaining points. Do not split initiatives or borrow capacity across teams or horizons. Every existing initiative remains visible, and the change request adds one initiative.

Apply hard commitments and dependency feasibility before advisory priority scores. A hard-deadline initiative may sit in the horizon containing its deadline or an earlier horizon, never later. An external dependency cannot support delivery before its current ready date. An initiative dependency must be in the same or an earlier horizon; when its prerequisite is externally blocked past a horizon, propagate that impact. Status changes should reflect the frozen dependency state. For any blocked or at-risk item, make the blocker, dependency owner, need-by timing, and usable contingency visible.

After those constraints, protect in-flight work and Must commitments, then use MoSCoW, RICE, strategic fit, and freed capacity to explain tradeoffs. RICE is advisory: small-reach contractual or compliance commitments can outrank it. The portfolio review needs to see what changed from the current roadmap, what was moved to make room, and which stakeholders absorb the impact.
