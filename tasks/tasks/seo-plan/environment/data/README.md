# ClosePilot SEO planning snapshot

All company, competitor, keyword, and site records here are fictional. The snapshot date is 2026-08-31; month 1 of the planning horizon starts 2026-09-01.

- `business_context.json`: business facts, baselines, targets, claim constraints, planning rules, and unit effort estimates.
- `keywords.csv`: 240 locally constructed keyword candidates grouped by `cluster_id`. `product_support` distinguishes current, planned, and beta topics.
- `existing_pages.csv`: the 120-row CMS/search/analytics inventory. Zero performance for draft pages is not a measured public result.
- `internal_links.csv`: exported internal links keyed to `page_id`; absence from `target_page_id` means no inbound links in this snapshot.
- `competitor_pages.csv`: 200 observed competitor-page records. `evidence_status` is the permitted freshness label; slugs are snapshot identifiers, not live URLs.
- `team_capacity.csv`: hard monthly role capacity before the required BAU reserve.
- `launch_calendar.csv`: product and campaign dependencies that constrain publication timing and claims.

Numeric traffic and conversion values cover the trailing 90 days. Keyword volumes are monthly. All hours are labor hours.
