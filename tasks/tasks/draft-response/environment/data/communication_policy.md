# Customer communication policy snapshot

Snapshot effective: 2026-09-09T12:30:00Z

- Incident communications may state externally approved symptoms, affected scope, current status, validated workarounds, and the next update time.
- Only the incident commander may authorize a resolution ETA or declare a root cause. No such authorization exists for INC-077.
- Account managers may acknowledge a service-credit request, but may not approve a credit or amount. Billing review BR-290 is pending.
- Exploratory roadmap work, internal project names, and unapproved engineering theories are confidential.
- A cancellation or renewal threat requires director review before the reply is sent.
