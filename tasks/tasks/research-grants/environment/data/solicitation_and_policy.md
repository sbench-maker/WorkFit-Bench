# Frozen solicitation and institutional policy snapshot

Use this local snapshot for this package; it overrides general assumptions. All entities and program details in the fixture are fictional.

## Solicitation rules

1. The project period is three years. The maximum total request, including indirect costs, is $1,100,000.
2. Voluntary committed cost sharing is prohibited unless the solicitation expressly requires it. This solicitation has no cost-sharing exception.
3. Across all NSF-funded work, each senior person may receive no more than 2.00 calendar months of compensated effort in a budget year without documented prior approval. No approval is present. When correcting this package, hold other NSF support fixed and reduce this proposal's requested months to the compliant maximum.
4. Each cost must occur in a year when its linked work-plan task is active. Dissemination Task D1 costs belong in Year 3.
5. Alcohol is unallowable and must not remain in the corrected request.
6. Tangible equipment costing at least $5,000 per unit must be classified as equipment. Equipment and participant support are excluded from the modified total direct cost base.
7. For each subaward, only the first $25,000 over the entire project is included in the modified total direct cost base. For the Cygnus subaward, apply that inclusion in Year 1; exclude all remaining Cygnus costs from the base.
8. Apply Northbridge's 54% on-campus research F&A rate to modified total direct costs. Round each personnel salary, fringe amount, annual F&A amount, and displayed annual total to the nearest dollar.
9. Required routing documents include a Data Management Plan. A one-page Postdoctoral Mentoring Plan is also required because a postdoc is supported. A named subaward requires its scope, detailed budget, and collaboration letter.

## Institutional costing and routing rules

- Escalate the Year 1 salary base by 3% in each later year. Salary equals the escalated annual base multiplied by requested calendar months divided by appointment months. Apply the fringe rate in `personnel_plan.csv` to the corrected rounded salary.
- The arithmetic authority for nonpersonnel detail is `quantity × unit_cost`; `proposed_amount` is the draft extension and may contain errors.
- Consultant rates above $1,500 per day may remain budgeted but the CV, signed commitment, and rate justification must be present before routing. Missing items are submission blockers.
- Current and pending support must be reconfirmed within seven calendar days of routing. Stale versions are “confirm later,” not blockers for today's budget reconciliation.
- Optional enhancement letters may remain pending and are not routing blockers.
- Treat missing required documents, unsupported voluntary cost sharing, and unresolved high-rate consultant documentation as blockers. Treat corrections already reflected in the reconciled budget as resolved items, not open blockers.
