# Legal, security, continuity, and financial diligence

## AtlasPay — V-ATLAS

- Current SOC 2 Type II report covers 2025-01-01 through 2025-12-31; the bridge letter is valid through 2026-12-31. No material exception was reported.
- Encryption: AES-256 at rest and TLS 1.2+ in transit. Production data is hosted in the United States.
- Draft DPA requires breach notice within 72 hours after AtlasPay confirms an incident; it sets no outer limit measured from discovery.
- New subprocessors require 15 days' notice. Northstar may object, but its only remedy is to stop the affected service.
- Customer assurance is limited to the SOC report and one annual questionnaire; the draft denies customer or third-party audit rights.
- Contractual recovery time objective (RTO) is eight hours and recovery point objective (RPO) is four hours. The latest annual recovery exercise restored service in 6 hours 20 minutes.
- AtlasPay reports annual revenue of $145 million, positive EBITDA of $12 million, and no going-concern qualification. Northstar represents about 0.4% of AtlasPay revenue.

## BeaconAP — V-BEACON

- Current SOC 2 Type II report covers 2025-06-01 through 2026-05-31; no material exception was reported. A bridge letter is due before production access.
- Encryption: AES-256 at rest and TLS 1.2+ in transit. Proposed production region is the United States.
- Draft DPA requires breach notice within 36 hours of discovery.
- New subprocessors require 30 days' notice and Northstar has a termination right if a reasonable objection cannot be resolved.
- Northstar receives current assurance reports and may commission an audit after a material incident or substantiated control concern.
- Contractual RTO is four hours and RPO is one hour. The latest recovery exercise restored service in 3 hours 45 minutes.
- BeaconAP reports annual revenue of $18 million, EBITDA of negative $7 million, cash of $11.7 million, and average monthly cash use of $0.65 million. Northstar would represent about 3.0% of revenue. No parent guarantee or source-code escrow is offered.

## Diligence note

Neither vendor reported a breach affecting Northstar data. BeaconAP's control evidence is adequate for diligence, but the bridge letter and custom-connector test results are still open items. Financial figures are vendor-supplied management figures and are unaudited.
