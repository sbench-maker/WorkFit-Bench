# BeaconAP replacement proposal — V-BEACON

## Commercial terms

- Three-year initial term, 2027-01-01 through 2029-12-31, non-cancellable after acceptance.
- 2027 platform subscription: $425,000, including 500,000 processed invoices.
- 2027 overage: $0.68 per invoice above the included volume.
- The platform subscription and overage rate increase 2% on each January 1 after 2027.
- Premium support is mandatory at $30,000 per year and does not escalate.
- One-time 2027 charges: implementation $95,000; Northstar LegacyERP connector and data migration $54,000; user/admin training $20,000.
- Northstar's required six-week dual-run adds $58,000 in transition cost.
- One standard-format export and 40 hours of exit assistance are included at term end; additional assistance is $250 per hour.
- Optional advanced supplier portal: $31,000 per year (not requested by Northstar).

## Service and support commitments

- Monthly availability SLA: 99.95%; credits start below 99.95% and rise to 12% of monthly subscription fees.
- Northstar may terminate for cause after three months below 99.90% in a rolling six-month period.
- Sev-1 first-response target: 20 minutes, backed by a service credit.
- Named escalation manager is included.

## Delivery and contract mechanics

- Proposed project runs 20 weeks from kickoff; to reach 2027-01-01, kickoff must occur by 2026-08-17. BeaconAP instead proposes 2026-10-12 kickoff and a 2027-03-01 production date.
- The LegacyERP integration is a custom connector and must pass volume and rollback testing.
- Convenience termination is unavailable during the initial term.
- Liability cap is 18 months of fees, with a separate two-times-fees cap for confidentiality and data-security claims.
