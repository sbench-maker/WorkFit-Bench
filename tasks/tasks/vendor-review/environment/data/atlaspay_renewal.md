# AtlasPay renewal offer — V-ATLAS

## Commercial terms

- Three-year renewal, 2027-01-01 through 2029-12-31.
- Usage fee in 2027: $0.82 per processed invoice, subject to a $360,000 annual minimum.
- The per-invoice fee and annual minimum increase 4% on each January 1 after 2027.
- Premium support is mandatory at $42,000 per year and does not escalate.
- Refresher training in 2027 is a one-time $8,000 charge.
- No implementation or migration fee for renewal.
- End-of-term standard data export is a mandatory $35,000 professional-services package, payable in 2029.
- Optional supplier-risk module: $26,000 per year (not requested by Northstar).

## Service and support commitments

- Monthly availability SLA: 99.90%.
- Service credits begin only when monthly availability is below 99.50%; the maximum credit is 5% of that month's usage fee.
- Sev-1 first-response target: 30 minutes, with no service credit for a missed response target.
- Dedicated escalation manager and quarterly release reviews are included.

## Contract mechanics

- Auto-renews for 12 months unless non-renewal notice is delivered 90 days before term end.
- Convenience termination is not available during the renewal term.
- Liability cap is fees paid in the preceding 12 months; confidentiality and data-security claims are not carved out.
- Export is delivered in AtlasPay's normalized CSV package; field mapping assistance is billed separately.
