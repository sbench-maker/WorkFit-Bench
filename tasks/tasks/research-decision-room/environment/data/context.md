# TalentLoop approval research snapshot

TalentLoop is a fictional recruiting-workflow product. This frozen snapshot covers 2026-08-03 through 2026-08-28 and was assembled locally for product planning; it is not a record of a real company or real customers.

## Decision in scope

Choose the best one-sprint improvement for requisition approvals: a shared status timeline, configurable automatic reminders, or clearer owner-and-due-date controls. The primary audience is the product trio and HR operations lead. The decision horizon is the September 2026 sprint.

## Constraints

- One product squad and one sprint; prefer a reversible test over a broad rebuild.
- Email-deliverability infrastructure and the permissions model are out of scope this sprint.
- Enterprise teams need timestamped approval history, but the team must also avoid burdening small accounts with a complex flow.
- No causal experiment has been run. Survey respondents were self-selected, support data overrepresents people who asked for help, and sales notes are summaries rather than transcripts.
- Product owner for an in-app experiment: Maya Chen. Research owner: Rowan Lee. Analytics owner: Eli Park. These are fictional staff names, not participants.

## File guide

- `interview_notes.md`: moderated interview notes and supplied verbatim quotes.
- `usability_sessions.md`: observed tasks in the current flow and two lightweight concepts.
- `support_tickets.csv`: 220 categorized ticket exports; blank `customer_words` means no direct quote was retained.
- `survey_responses.csv`: 72 self-selected responses.
- `workflow_metrics.csv`: frozen aggregate product metrics with explicit limitations.
- `sales_notes.csv`: 18 account-call summaries, not direct transcripts.
- `stakeholder_notes.md`: internal opinions and constraints; these are context, not user evidence.
