# Dispatch Windows launch notes

Status labels matter. This is a working document, not the approval source.

## Approved positioning

- Product name: Dispatch Windows.
- Plain-language promise: give dispatchers a shared view of customer promise windows and the slack around them before they move work.
- General availability: June 18, 2026, on Growth and Scale plans.
- The product recommends feasible moves. A dispatcher remains responsible for reassignment, and the system does not contact customers automatically.

## Draft demo sequence

1. Open a work order with a saved 10 a.m.–noon customer promise.
2. Show the route view flagging that a proposed move would break the promise.
3. Compare a second move that preserves the promise and fits technician availability.
4. Confirm the move; the existing technician mobile view updates.

## Internal targets — may be published only as targets

- Sustain at least a 25% reduction in median active scheduling time per work order after launch.
- Keep after-hours reassignment below 8%.

## Superseded or unapproved — do not publish

- Superseded launch date: June 12, 2026.
