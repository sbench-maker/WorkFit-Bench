# Planning-session notes

The leadership team wants a method that can survive at least two quarterly cycles; changing the framework again next month would damage trust. The monthly review should produce a shortlist for quarterly planning, not an automatic shipping order.

The PM can facilitate a 60-minute calibration session with product, design, engineering, sales, and support. Participants will accept explicit weights and shared definitions if the method distinguishes evidence quality from stakeholder volume. They do not want eight or ten criteria.

Items with legal, security, or signed contractual deadlines need a visible gate before comparative scoring. A small number of strategic learning bets may also require a documented exception. All other items should be compared consistently, with an owner and review date for any override.

The team is willing to pilot a spreadsheet template for one monthly review, retrospect on disagreements, then lock definitions and weights for the following two quarters.
