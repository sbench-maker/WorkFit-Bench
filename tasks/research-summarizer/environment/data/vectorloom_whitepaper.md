## The handoff opportunity

Support organizations lose valuable time when frontline agents must reconstruct a customer story. SynapseNote Assist creates a proposed escalation summary from conversation history and ticket fields.

Across participating customers, teams using SynapseNote reported faster escalations and more consistent notes. The sponsor describes the product as ready to transform every support workflow.

This white paper reports an average 31 percent reduction in self-reported handoff preparation time. It recommends broad deployment of assisted summaries.

## Customer sample

VectorLoom invited current SynapseNote customers to submit usage summaries and answer a survey. Sixty-five customer organizations participated, contributing aggregate information about approximately 78,000 handoffs.

Participants ranged from 20 to 4,000 support agents. Industry and region counts are not supplied. Customer names are withheld under commercial agreements.

Nine invited customers that had paused or ended deployment were excluded because the sponsor classified their usage data as incomplete. The response rate among the remaining invited customers is not stated.

## Measurement approach

Each customer selected its own four-week baseline and four-week comparison period. Customers reported average preparation time from internal dashboards or manager estimates.

The paper pooled customer-level percentage changes without weighting by handoff volume. There was no concurrent manual-note control group and no independent verification of submitted figures.

Some customers required agent review; others allowed notes to move directly into an escalation queue. The white paper combines those workflows and does not report results separately.

The survey asked managers whether note quality was better, unchanged, or worse. It did not define factual accuracy or inspect notes against source conversations.

## Results

The unweighted mean customer-reported reduction in handoff preparation time was 31 percent. The median reduction, distribution, and uncertainty interval are not provided.

Eighty-two percent of responding managers rated note consistency as better. The denominator for this survey item is 51 managers, not all 65 organizations.

The dataset represented approximately 78,000 handoffs, but six large organizations contributed 71 percent of that volume. Because the analysis averages organizations equally, that concentration is not reflected in the headline figure.

No factual-error rate, resolution-time outcome, reopened-case outcome, or customer-harm measure is reported.

## Recommendation

VectorLoom recommends activating SynapseNote Assist across all standard support queues and refining templates after launch. Organizations may choose their preferred review policy.

The recommendation does not discuss high-severity exclusions, rollback thresholds, or the difference between a reviewed draft and automatic publication.
